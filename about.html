Our story

From a Kuri Road kitchen to Islamabad's celebration table.

Oasis began in 1988 with a single deg and a simple belief — food served with love tastes different. Today we cater weddings, corporate evenings and family gatherings across Islamabad and beyond, with our own marquees, décor and service teams.

No middlemen, no surprises: you deal directly with the family that cooks your food and sets your stage.
