Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Oasis Catering and Event Management (“Oasis”, “we”) respects your privacy. This website requires no login, signup or account of any kind.

1. Information you share with us

When you request a quotation or message us, you may share your name, phone number, email, event details and menu preferences. This happens through your own WhatsApp, email app or phone call — your message goes directly to our team so we can respond to your enquiry.

2. What we do with it

  • We use your details only to prepare quotations, plan events and communicate with you.
  • We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We keep correspondence only as long as needed for your event and any follow-up.

3. What we don't collect

We operate no customer accounts, member areas or dashboards. We set no advertising trackers of our own. Your menu shortlist is stored only in your own browser (local storage) and you can clear it anytime by clearing site data.

4. Third-party services

Pages may load fonts, images, maps or social links from third parties (e.g. Google Fonts, Google Maps, Unsplash, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp). Those providers apply their own privacy policies when you use their services or follow outbound links.

5. Your choices

Prefer not to write? Simply call 0329 5977659. To ask us to delete correspondence, email oasiscatering.pk@gmail.com from the address you wrote from.
